Privacy notice

Last updated 7 October 2026

In short: if you join the waitlist, we keep your name and email so we can tell you when the full game opens. The early-access game stores your progress on your own device and sends nothing to us. We don't sell your details, and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

The waitlist ("MAKE I JOIN")

  • What we collect: your first name, last name, email address, and your gender if you choose to give it.
  • Why: to email you when the full game opens and to send your invite. Nothing else.
  • Where it's kept: in our database, run for us by Supabase.
  • How long: until you join the game or ask us to remove you.

The early-access game (naschool.app/play)

  • There's no account and no password. Your student, progress and settings are saved in your browser on your device.
  • None of that is sent to us. If you clear your browser data, your save is gone.
  • The other students you meet in early access are computer-controlled (marked NPC). There's no chat with real people yet.
  • Early access is for players aged 18 and over.

Cookies

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies on this site. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may use strictly necessary security measures to keep the site running and protect it from attacks.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email naschoolhq@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

When this changes

The full multiplayer game will need accounts and will store more, such as your progress and chat for safety. We'll update this notice and tell you before we collect anything new.
